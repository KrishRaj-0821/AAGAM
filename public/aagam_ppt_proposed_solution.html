SLIDE 3 — PILLAR 1: FARMER REGISTRATION & VERIFICATION

Instant Aadhaar e-KYC & PM-KISAN Land Record Integration

  • Verhoeff Aadhaar Checksum: Verifies 12-digit Indian Aadhaar numbers without storing sensitive plain-text UIDs.
  • PM-KISAN Land Registry: Verifies land holdings (Khatauni) directly to prevent unauthorized trader entries.
  • Strict Data Validation: Enforces 10-digit mobile number rules with zero dummy defaults.
  • Bilingual UI: 100% instant translation support between English and हिंदी (t(en, hi)).
SLIDE 4 — PILLAR 2: SMART SLOT BOOKING & QR GATE PASS

Dynamic Appointment Scheduling & Encrypted QR Passes

  • 1-Hour Arrival Time Windows: Farmers select produce quantity and preferred center, receiving exact time slots.
  • Encrypted QR Gate Pass: Encodes Farmer ID, Lot ID, Vehicle Number, and Dedicated Mandi Lane.
  • Center Intake Cap: Prevents physical bottlenecks by dynamic capacity load balancing.
SLIDE 5 — PILLAR 3: REAL-TIME VIRTUAL QUEUE MANAGEMENT

Congestion Reduction & Express Vehicle Routing

  • Live Virtual Queue Tracker: Real-time dashboard showing truck counts, active weighbridge scales, and queue status.
  • Digitized Tola Parchi: Direct digital scale integration eliminates manual weight tampering.
  • Result: Cuts procurement center waiting time from 48 hours to under 45 minutes (-90%).
SLIDE 6 — PILLAR 4: AUTOMATED MULTI-CHANNEL NOTIFICATIONS

SMS, Email, WhatsApp & Printable PDF Receipts

  • Instant Multi-Channel Dispatch: Automated SMS, Email, WhatsApp, and App push notifications.
  • Official PDF Certificates: Issues plain-text (.txt) and official printable PDF receipts (window.print()) with NIC verification seals.
SLIDE 7 — PILLAR 5: AI QUALITY ASSAY & DBT PAYMENT TRACKING

15-Second AI Quality Grading & Direct Bank Transfers

  • AI NIR Quality Agent: Emulates Near-Infrared spectroscopy to check moisture %, broken kernels, and visual grade.
  • NPCI-DBT Direct Settlement: 100% bank credit into the farmer's SBI account (XXXX 4892) within 24-48 hours.
  • PFMS & UTR Tracking: Real-time tracking of UTR numbers (SBIN0048299104) and PFMS IDs.
  • 3-Tier Support Dashboard: Categorizes tickets into 🟢 Problem Solved, 🟡 Processing, and 🔴 Not Resolved.
SLIDE 8 — IMPACT & KEY DIFFERENTIATORS

Why AAGAM is the Winning Solution

  • 90% Congestion Reduction: Mandi wait times drop from 48 hours to <45 minutes.
  • Zero Intermediary Cut: 100% direct bank transfer via NPCI-DBT gateway.
  • Instant Quality Assay: Objective AI NIR grading in 15 seconds.
  • 24-Hour Grievance SLA: Transparent tracking under Problem Solved status columns.
