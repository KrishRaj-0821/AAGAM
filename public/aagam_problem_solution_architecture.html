1. Key Agricultural Challenges vs. AAGAM Technical Solutions
🔴 Traditional Agricultural Bottlenecks (Problems) 🟢 AAGAM Architectural Solutions
Long Mandi Queues: Farmers wait 48+ hours in truck queues outside APMC mandis without arrival visibility.
Virtual Queue & QR Token Engine: Algorithmic slot booking assigns exact 1-hour arrival windows and issues digital QR Gate Passes.
Subjective Manual Quality Grading: Manual grain grading by commission agents leads to unfair price cuts and disputes.
AI Visual Scanner & NIR Spectroscopy: Objective 15-second visual camera scan & moisture meter grading (Grade A/B/C).
Payment Delays & Deductions: Settlements take weeks or months through intermediaries with unauthorized commission cuts.
NPCI-DBT Settlement Engine: Automatic Direct Bank Transfer credited directly to the farmer's SBI account in 24-48h via PFMS.
Price Opacity & Distress Sales: Farmers lack real-time price awareness, selling below Minimum Support Price (MSP).
Agmarknet Live Ticker & Price Matrix: Real-time APMC price benchmarks comparing regional mandi rates vs GOI MSP floors.
Unresolved Grievances: Scale discrepancies or slot issues have no escalation path or tracking.
3-Tier Grievance Status Dashboard: Categorizes tickets into Problem Solved, Processing, and Open Dispute with SLA tracking.
Warehouse Spoilage: Harvested grain rots out in open CAP storage due to lack of warehouse allocation visibility.
Predictive WDRA Warehouse Allocation: Real-time storage capacity tracking, e-NWR creation, and automated truck dispatching.
