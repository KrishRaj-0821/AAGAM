Workflow Phase System Action & Module Input / Output Data
1. Registration & Profile Farmer Registration (Aadhaar e-KYC) 12-digit Aadhaar Checksum, PM-KISAN Khatauni land verification.
2. Produce Lot Creation Produce Lot Creation Module Crop Type, Variety, Harvest Date, Quantity (KG / Qtl).

6. Procurement & Sales Live E-Auction & FCI Direct Procurement Agmarknet MSP Floor Rate, Bidding Ledger, Procurement Certificate.
7. Payment & Support NPCI-DBT Payment Terminal & Grievance Desk PFMS UTR Direct Credit (₹1,23,500), 3-Tier Support Ticket Tracking.
