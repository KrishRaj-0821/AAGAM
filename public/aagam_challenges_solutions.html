CRITICAL BOTTLENECK
Unregulated Mandi Congestion
Producers encounter multi-day queue delays outside APMC yards due to uncoordinated physical truck arrivals.
ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION
Virtual Queue & QR Token Engine
Establishes automated intake load balancing with 1-hour time windows and encrypted QR Gate Pass credentials.
CRITICAL BOTTLENECK
Subjective Quality Assessment
Manual visual inspection introduces arbitrary price deductions and moisture measurement disputes at procurement gates.
ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION
AI NIR Spectroscopy Assay
Executes objective grain quality grading (Grade A/B/C) within 15 seconds backed by Merkle Tree audit logs.
CRITICAL BOTTLENECK
Extended Settlement Liquidity Delays
Financial disbursements experience multi-week processing lags through traditional commission agent channels.
ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION
NPCI-DBT Settlement Terminal
Automates direct electronic funds credit to beneficiary bank accounts within 24–48 hours via PFMS with UTR tracking.
CRITICAL BOTTLENECK
Market Price Asymmetry
Producers lack real-time price intelligence across regional APMCs, leading to distress transactions below support benchmarks.
ARCHITECTURAL SOLUTION
Agmarknet Real-Time Matrix
Synchronizes live APMC market rates against Government Minimum Support Price (MSP) regulatory benchmarks.
